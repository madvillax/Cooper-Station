CS-01 FIELD GUIDE

Mission Brief

Cooper Station is a fictional settlement inspired by Gerard O’Neill’s rotating space habitats. Its farms, river and settlements line the inner wall, so the landscape curves up and overhead.

Orbital
Drag to orbit the station. Scroll or pinch to change your distance.
Cutaway
Look through a section of the outer hull to see the full landscape.
Habitat
Fly through the docking aperture, then use WASD or the on-screen arrows to move. Drag or use the arrow keys to look around.
Rotation
Change the spin to see the gravity at the rim. Gravity rises with the square of the spin and falls toward zero at the axis. The time multiplier changes playback speed.

The habitat dimensions are illustrative. Rim gravity uses a radius of 3.2 km and Earth gravity as its reference.
